Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 21, 2026

Wandr is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it. We keep it simple — no jargon, no surprises.

01

Who we are

Wandr ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered travel itinerary planning application available at wandr-app-eight.vercel.app. Wandr is operated by an independent developer and is not affiliated with any travel booking service.

02

Information we collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and profile picture, obtained via Google Sign-In when you create an account.
  • Username — a username you choose during onboarding, used to identify you when sharing itineraries.
  • Trip data — itineraries you generate, including destination, dates, preferences, and the AI-generated content.
  • Usage data — basic information about how you interact with the app, such as pages visited and features used.
We do not collect: payment information, precise location data, contacts, or any sensitive personal information.

03

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to provide and improve Wandr:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To generate and save your travel itineraries
  • To display your username when you share itineraries
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

04

AI-generated content

Wandr uses AI to generate travel itineraries based on your inputs (destination, dates, interests, budget). Your prompts are sent to an AI provider to generate itinerary content.

Important: Do not include sensitive personal information in your travel planning inputs. Treat your destination and travel preferences as the only necessary inputs.

AI-generated itineraries are suggestions only. Wandr does not guarantee the accuracy of any specific recommendation and is not responsible for any decisions made based on generated content.

05

Data storage and security

Your account and trip data are stored securely using Supabase, a trusted database platform with encryption at rest and in transit. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth in conjunction with Google OAuth.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure. We will notify you of any breach that affects your personal information.

06

Third-party services

Wandr uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google OAuth — for authentication (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Supabase — for database and authentication (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Vercel — for hosting (vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)
  • Anthropic / Google AI — for AI itinerary generation

07

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your trip data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account

You can delete your account directly in the app via Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes all your data from our systems.

08

Cookies

Wandr uses only essential session cookies required for authentication. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

09

Children's privacy

Wandr is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Wandr after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions about your privacy?

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, please reach out — we'll respond within 48 hours.
